### The inventories of loot containers are unique to every player who opens them.

Players can explore structures together without fighting over the loot. Since players are encouraged to revisit existing structures rather than generate new ones, Unique Loot also helps keep the world size small.

You can find more information about the plugin, as well as the download, on Modrinth: https://modrinth.com/plugin/unique-loot
